About

I'm a third-year computer science student at the Lebanese University, working full-stack across ASP.NET Core, React, and SQL Server. I like projects with a real user on the other end — a studio owner trying to stop double-booking a room, a shop trying to understand its own numbers.

Outside of coursework, I work sales and customer support for musical instruments and audio gear. It's given me an unreasonably specific appreciation for margins, inventory, and the gap between a good product and a product that actually sells — which is most of why I'm drawn to data analytics and AI automation now, not just writing more front end.
